Made in the garage

Cut, engraved, sealed — by hand.

No factory, no warehouse. Every coaster runs through the laser in a garage shop in Richardson, Texas, and gets packed the same day it’s engraved.

01 · Set

Set on the bed

Each slate is wiped clean, jigged flat, and lined up on the laser bed — six at a time, one pack per run.

02 · Engrave

Etched, not printed

The laser etches the design into the stone itself. No ink, no vinyl — nothing to fade, peel, or wash off.

03 · Seal & number

Sealed and numbered

A matte sealer goes on after engraving, the pack gets its number, and it’s boxed for shipping within 24 hours.
